# World map coastlines

Source: [Natural Earth 1:110m land, v5.1.2](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/v5.1.2/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson).

Natural Earth data is [public domain](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/).
The bundled `world-land.json` retains polygon rings as longitude/latitude pairs,
rounded to three decimal places. It contains no political boundaries and works
offline, without sending photo locations to a third-party map provider.
